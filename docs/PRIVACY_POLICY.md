# Privacy Policy for QuizForge AI

**Last Updated:** September 10, 2026  
**Effective Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Package:** `com.sachinkumar.quizforge.quizforge_upsc`

QuizForge AI ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an enterprise-grade Learning Operating System and exam preparation tool designed for civil services aspirants. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal and educational data processed by the Application.

---

## 1. Information We Process

### 1.1 Personal Information (Optional)
- **Account Name and Email**: If provided during profile customization, this information is stored locally on your device for user experience personalization.
- **No Mandatory Registration**: QuizForge AI supports a full guest / offline mode that does not require an account or registration.

### 1.2 Educational and Learning Activity
- **Quiz Responses, Assessment Attempts, and Scores**: Processed locally on your device to calculate mastery scores, generate Bloom's taxonomy analytics, and maintain study streaks.
- **Transcripts and Certificates**: Generated and validated cryptographically on-device or via the authenticated enterprise learning backend.

### 1.3 AI Study Mentor Prompts (Optional)
- **User Queries**: When you submit questions to the AI Study Mentor, the query text is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS to the configured AI inference backend (e.g., Google Gemini) solely to generate the educational response.
- **Zero Advertising or Profiling**: Queries are never used for advertising, behavioral targeting, or third-party profiling.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Device Identifiers or Advertising IDs**: We do not collect AAID, IMEI, MAC addresses, or persistent advertising identifiers.
- **No Location Data**: We do not request or access GPS, cellular, or Wi-Fi location.
- **No Financial Data**: The Application is free; no credit card or billing details are collected.
- **No Device Sensors or Media**: We do not access contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or storage outside the app sandbox.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Security, and Offline-First Architecture
- **Offline-First Storage**: All educational activity and practice data are stored locally in on-device storage.
- **Encryption in Transit**: All communications with the production backend (`https://api.quizforge.ai`) or AI APIs use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Cleartext HTTP traffic is strictly forbidden by Android network security configuration.
- **No Third-Party Trackers**: The Application contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or social media trackers.

---

## 4. User Control and Data Deletion
- You retain complete control over your educational data.
- You can clear all learning history, quiz attempts, and cached credentials at any time via **Settings -> Reset Local Data**.
- Uninstalling the Application completely removes all locally stored data from your device.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
QuizForge AI is designed for adult students and civil service examination aspirants (aged 18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
