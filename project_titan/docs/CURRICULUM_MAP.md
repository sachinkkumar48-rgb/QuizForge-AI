# Curriculum Map: UPSC Civil Services – Indian Polity Foundation

| Module # | Module Title | Chapters Count | Lessons Count | Duration | Key Exam Topics Covered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Module 1** | Historical Background | 2 | 4 | 360 mins | Regulating Act 1773, Pitt's India Act 1784, Charter Acts, GoI Acts 1858/1919/1935, Independence Act 1947 |
| **Module 2** | Making of the Constitution | 1 | 2 | 300 mins | Cabinet Mission Plan, Constituent Assembly, Objectives Resolution, Drafting Committee, Enactment |
| **Module 3** | Salient Features | 1 | 2 | 240 mins | Written Constitution, Rigidity vs Flexibility, Borrowed Sources, Parliamentary Form, Quasi-Federal System |
| **Module 4** | Preamble | 1 | 2 | 240 mins | Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic, Republic, Berubari, Kesavananda Bharati & LIC rulings |
| **Module 5** | Union & Territory | 1 | 2 | 240 mins | Articles 1-4, Dhar & JVP Commissions, Fazl Ali Commission, States Reorganisation Act 1956 |
| **Module 6** | Citizenship | 1 | 2 | 240 mins | Articles 5-11, Citizenship Act 1955, Acquisition/Loss, OCI, CAA 2019 provisions |
| **Module 7** | Fundamental Rights | 2 | 4 | 480 mins | Articles 12-35, Right to Equality, Freedom, Life & Liberty (Art 21), Article 32 Writs, Basic Structure |
| **Module 8** | Directive Principles (DPSP) | 1 | 2 | 300 mins | Articles 36-51, Socialistic/Gandhian/Liberal principles, FR vs DPSP Conflict, Minerva Mills Doctrine |
| **Module 9** | Fundamental Duties | 1 | 2 | 240 mins | Article 51A, Swaran Singh Committee, 42nd & 86th Amendments, 11 Duties, Verma Committee |
| **Module 10** | Amendment Procedure | 1 | 2 | 300 mins | Article 368, Simple/Special majorities, State ratification, Scope of amending power, Basic Structure |

**Total Curriculum Weight**: 10 Modules | 11 Chapters | 24 Lessons | 49 Hours Core Direct Instruction
