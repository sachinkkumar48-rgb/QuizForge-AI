3. Compare writ jurisdiction of Supreme Court (Art 32) vs High Courts (Art 226).

## Lesson Content
Article 32 provides the right to move the Supreme Court by appropriate proceedings for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights. Supreme Court has the power to issue directions, orders, or writs.

### 5 Types of Writs
1. **Habeas Corpus** ("To have the body of"): Protects individual liberty against arbitrary detention by State or private entity.
2. **Mandamus** ("We command"): Commands a public official or body to perform a mandatory statutory duty.
3. **Prohibition**: Issued by higher court to lower court/tribunal to prevent it from exceeding its jurisdiction.
4. **Certiorari** ("To be certified"): Quashes an order already passed by an inferior court or quasi-judicial body without jurisdiction.
5. **Quo-Warranto** ("By what authority"): Inquires into the legality of a person's claim to a public office.
